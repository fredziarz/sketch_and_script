Lake Apartments

Residential Development

A new residential development on the edge of Lake Tegel in Berlin. The site sits right at the water — the buildings are positioned to make the most of that, with lake views from the main living spaces.

Large windows run the full height of the living rooms. The palette is kept neutral to let the water and light do most of the work.

Completed 2020 • Berlin, Germany
